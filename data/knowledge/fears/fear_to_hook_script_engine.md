# Fear to Hook Script Engine

## Purpose
Turn a single renovation fear into immediate, usable marketing and sales assets.

## Input
A single fear file from `data/knowledge/fears/`.

## Output Required
For the selected fear, always generate:
1. Five ad hooks
2. One short-form video script
3. One VSL pain/proof segment
4. Three objections with responses

## Rules
- Use UK English
- Speak to UK homeowners
- Keep language plain and natural
- Avoid hypey American phrasing
- Focus on emotional truth, not fluff
- Where possible, include one statistic from the source fear file
- Tie the pain to a practical consequence in family life, money, timing, stress, or decision-making
- Tie the solution back to structure, clarity, expertise, and reduced risk

## Output Structure

### 1. Five Ad Hooks
Generate:
- one pain-led hook
- one stat-led hook
- one story-led hook
- one contrarian hook
- one fear-escalation hook

### 2. Short-form Video Script
Generate a 30-60 second script with:
- Hook
- Problem
- Consequence
- Reframe
- CTA

CTA should usually point to:
- booking a free call
- downloading a checklist
- watching a longer explainer

### 3. VSL Pain/Proof Segment
Generate:
- one pain paragraph
- one proof paragraph
- one transition into the solution

### 4. Three Objections with Responses
Format as:
- Objection
- What they really mean
- Strong response

## Angle Guidance

### If the fear is cost-related
Emphasise:
- hidden costs
- budget drift
- loss of financial control
- wasted spend

### If the fear is planning-related
Emphasise:
- uncertainty
- council delays
- refusal risk
- not knowing what is actually possible

### If the fear is people-related
Emphasise:
- trust
- coordination
- poor communication
- lack of accountability

### If the fear is psychological
Emphasise:
- overwhelm
- delay
- indecision
- family stress

## CTA Guidance
Use a low-pressure CTA.
Examples:
- Book a free Build, Wait or Move call
- Get clear on what is possible before you spend money in the wrong place
- Start with a plan before you commit to the build

## Quality Control
Before finalising, check:
- Does this sound like a real homeowner problem?
- Is the fear specific enough?
- Is the consequence tangible?
- Is the solution credible, not magical?
- Would this help someone move from confusion to action?
