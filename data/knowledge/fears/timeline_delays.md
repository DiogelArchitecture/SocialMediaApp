# Project Timeline Delays in UK Home Renovations

## Executive Summary
91% of UK renovation projects experience timeline delays, making this the most widespread and predictable issue homeowners face.

## Key Statistics
- 91% of projects delayed
- Average overrun: 62.5%
- Kitchen planning delays up to 450%
- 75% of homeowners experience stress
- 58% regret starting their renovation

## Core Problem
Delays are not exceptions. They are the norm. The system itself is slow and unpredictable, from planning through to construction.

## Root Causes
### Planning system bottlenecks
- Validation delays
- Consultation extensions
- Backlogs in decision making

### Labour shortages
- Lack of skilled trades
- Scheduling conflicts

### Supply chain issues
- Material delays
- Manufacturing delays
- Logistics challenges

## Psychological Impact
- Loss of control
- Financial anxiety increases
- Decision fatigue worsens over time
- Family disruption from extended timelines

## Financial Impact
- Cost escalation
- Temporary accommodation costs
- Lost opportunity value

## Strategic Use
Use this for:
- urgency messaging
- expectation setting
- positioning your process as structured and controlled
- showing why planning properly matters
