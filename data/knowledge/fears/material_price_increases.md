# Material Price Increases in UK Home Renovations

## Executive Summary
Material cost volatility has become a major risk factor in UK renovations, making early-stage budgeting unreliable.

## Key Statistics
- Material costs increased significantly post-2020
- Timber, steel, and insulation saw double-digit inflation spikes
- Supply chain disruption continues to affect availability

## Core Problem
Even fixed quotes can become unstable due to changing material costs.

## Main Pain Points
- Budget uncertainty
- Delays due to material shortages
- Need to substitute materials mid-project

## Strategic Use
Use this for:
- explaining why early planning matters
- reinforcing realistic budgeting
- positioning proactive design as cost control
