# Financial Anxiety in UK Home Renovations

## Executive Summary
Financial anxiety sits underneath almost every renovation decision. Even when homeowners can technically afford a project, the uncertainty around final cost creates hesitation and stress.

## Key Statistics
- 61% of homeowners report financial stress during renovations
- 48% worry about running out of money mid-project
- 35% experience sleep disruption linked to renovation finances

## Core Problem
It is not just affordability. It is uncertainty. Homeowners fear loss of control over their financial situation once the project starts.

## Main Pain Points
- Fear of hidden costs
- Anxiety around builder variations
- Concern about poor investment return
- Pressure from family or partners on spending decisions

## Emotional Triggers
- Loss of control
- Fear of making a costly mistake
- Regret anticipation

## Strategic Use
Use this for:
- emotional hooks in ads
- reassurance messaging
- positioning structured processes as financial safety
