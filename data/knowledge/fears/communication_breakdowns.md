# Communication Breakdowns in UK Home Renovations

## Executive Summary
Poor communication is a major contributor to failed or stressful renovation projects.

## Core Problem
Multiple stakeholders with no central control leads to misalignment.

## Main Pain Points
- Misunderstood requirements
- Delays caused by unclear instructions
- Disputes between homeowner and contractor

## Strategic Use
Use this for:
- emphasising structured communication
- positioning process clarity as a benefit
