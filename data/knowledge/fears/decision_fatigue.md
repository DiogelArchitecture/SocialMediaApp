# Decision Fatigue in UK Home Renovations

## Executive Summary
Renovations require hundreds of decisions, many of which homeowners are not equipped to make confidently.

## Core Problem
Too many decisions without enough guidance leads to overwhelm and delays.

## Main Pain Points
- Choosing layouts, materials, finishes
- Conflicting advice from different professionals
- Fear of making irreversible mistakes

## Effects
- Delays in approvals
- Reduced satisfaction with outcomes
- Increased stress

## Strategic Use
Use this for:
- positioning guidance as valuable
- content about simplifying decisions
